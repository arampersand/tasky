Install

Flash the latest firmware

Straight from this page over USB. No toolchain, no drivers on macOS/Linux/Windows 10+.

  1. 1

    Pick your firmware and board

  2. 2

    Plug it in

    Use a USB-C data cable. If no port shows up, hold BOOT on the XIAO while plugging it in.

    ✓ Your Tasky is plugged in.

  3. 3

    Flash

    Your browser can't talk to USB devices. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer. Web Serial needs a secure page — open https://tasky-flash.vercel.app.

    The browser lists only Tasky-type devices. Pick USB JTAG/serial debug unit: that's your Tasky.

    Erase device in the installer only wipes Tasky's own memory (Wi-Fi, tasks, settings). Leave it off to keep them. It can't touch your disks or anything else on your computer.

    No firmware release has been published yet. Check back soon or build it yourself.

Connect to your Tasky

Close the installer window if it's still open, then pick USB JTAG/serial debug unit in the list. That's your Tasky.

Prefer your phone? On Tasky open More → Setup (QR) and scan the code.

Wi-Fi

Tasky uses Wi-Fi for the time, weather and your calendar. 2.4 GHz networks only.

Your calendar

Paste your calendar's private iCal link. Today's events show up on Tasky, with a heads-up 5 minutes before each meeting.

Where do I find it?

Google Calendar: Settings → your calendar → Integrate calendar → copy Secret address in iCal format.

Outlook: Settings → Calendar → Shared calendars → Publish a calendar → copy the ICS link.

iCloud: Calendar app → share the calendar as Public Calendar → copy the link (change webcal:// to https://).

Today's three things

Tasky keeps it to three. You can change them any time.

GitHub streak

Optional. Your public contributions count toward Tasky's streak.

Ask Tasky

Optional. Add a ChatGPT or Claude API key for the Agent screen. The key stays on your Tasky.

Get one at platform.openai.com or console.anthropic.com.

Weather

Use your location, or type a city.

Light

Which light does your Tasky have? It blinks once when you save.

Hand-built and v1.1 boards have plain LEDs. The v3.1 board has the RGB bar along the top.

Your Tasky is ready ✨

  • Home shows the time, weather, what's next and your tasks.
  • Press the knob to start focusing. Press again to pause.
  • MODE moves to the next screen; double-press goes back.

Connect your agents: keep Tasky plugged into this computer and add the Claude Code hooks. Tasky's light breathes when an agent needs you, and the Controls screen turns the knob into a volume dial once you pair "Tasky" in Bluetooth.

Setup over USB needs Chrome or Edge on a computer. On your phone, open More → Setup (QR) on Tasky and scan the code.

Already running Tasky? You can also update over Wi-Fi: open More → Update on the device, then upload the release's …-ota.bin from its setup page. Reinstalling here keeps your settings unless you choose to erase.

What it does

Calm tech for deep work

Focus timer

Pomodoro-style sessions with a draining ring. The light bar turns on so people around you know you're heads-down.

Today's tasks

Three tasks, no more. Track focus minutes per task and tick them off with a click.

Streak

A GitHub-style grid fed by your focus sessions and, optionally, your real GitHub contributions.

Calendar & agenda

Reads any iCal feed. Warns you 5 minutes before a meeting and gives you a morning brief.

Weather

Today at a glance, no API key needed.

AI agent

Bring your Claude or OpenAI key for a pocket assistant on your desk.
