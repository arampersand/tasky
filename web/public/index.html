After flashing

  1. On Tasky, double-press MODE → More → Setup (QR) to turn on the setup network.
  2. Scan the QR, or join Wi-Fi Tasky with the password shown on screen (every Tasky has its own). The setup page opens by itself — or go to 192.168.4.1.
  3. Add your Wi-Fi and, if you like, your calendar, GitHub, Notion, weather and AI keys.

Already running Tasky? You can also update over Wi-Fi: open More → Update on the device, then upload the release's …-ota.bin from the setup page. Reinstalling here keeps your settings unless you choose to erase.

What it does

Calm tech for deep work

Focus timer

Pomodoro-style sessions with a draining ring. The light bar turns on so people around you know you're heads-down.

Today's tasks

Three tasks, no more. Track focus minutes per task and tick them off with a click.

Streak

A GitHub-style grid fed by your focus sessions and, optionally, your real GitHub contributions.

Calendar & agenda

Reads any iCal feed. Warns you 5 minutes before a meeting and gives you a morning brief.

Weather

Today at a glance, no API key needed.

AI agent

Bring your Claude or OpenAI key for a pocket assistant on your desk.
